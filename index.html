01

Preparations for the Run

Choose a team you can move and fight comfortably with. Fast movement helps you cover the map before bosses transform. Pyrois is an option for finding loot: he reveals chests on the map, reducing the need to check every corner for spawns.

My loadout

Here’s what I bring into a run.

6 keycards for Suburb Illusion

BRING 3

Etheric Phantom: Thracian

I bring this to quickly break a boss’s Cacophony Shield—the teal bar below its health.

I open with R to jump onto the boss, then use Thracian once its shield appears. This can finish the fight very quickly; on the hardest difficulty, the boss may still have roughly 10% HP left.

The item deals 100% / 50% / 15% of maximum HP to normal / elite / boss enemies and depletes 100% of their Cacophony Shield. The Hound has a special exception; do not assume its shield is fully removed.

BRING 9

Etheric Phantom: Golden Bangboo

Use this to search several containers quickly. Searching takes 30% of the normal time, and the transformation protects you from Corruption caused by Cacophony containers.

Kill the nearby boss first, then transform. You can search its loot sphere and the nearby chests during the same use. I use this to loot clusters of three or four chests in roughly 25–30 seconds, including slow-to-reveal red items.

! TIP

The Golden Bangboo can be used to loot chests while Hound Ulidim is chasing you. The two main rules involve Ulidim's line of sight and the 10-minute timer rule.

If Ulidim has spawned but hasn't seen you yet, or if you can break his direct line of sight for at least one second, use the Bangboo transformation and loot the chests. He may come close, but he will not attack unless there is less than 10:00 remaining on the global timer or you switch back to your character.

BRING 3

Corruption Cleanse Medicine

Bring this as a backup for maximum HP lost to Corruption, especially if the Hound hits you early.

It restores 20% of each squad member’s lost maximum HP over one second. Taking a hit interrupts the effect. It also protects the squad from the next Corruption increase caused by a Corruption Ambush within 60 seconds.

I often finish with all three unused, but I like having them as a backup.

BRING 3

Revival Medicine

Use this for healing and to negate one instance of damage within 60 seconds. I use that protection to absorb the Hound’s ultimate when I enter its arena.

It restores 50% of the active Agent’s maximum HP over three seconds; taking a hit interrupts healing. Using another dose resets the recovery and damage-immunity durations.

A note on controls

I use R for the opening action on PC. If your controls differ, use the corresponding action on your setup.

02

Build Your Routing

Before you move, check your spawn and identify which of the four marked bosses you can reach first. These bosses can transform into small Hounds when their zone becomes corrupted, costing you their original loot.

25:00 → 20:00Your opening priority window

For bosses in a zone affected by the first Corruption wave, finish the fight before the timer drops below 20:00.

At around 19:59, an affected boss can already be gone. Starting the fight before the cutoff is not enough: it can disappear during combat if you fail to kill it in time.

Plan around the bosses, then the loot

Your route may double back or use a portal instead of following a simple loop. The purpose is to secure boss kills before Corruption reaches them. See the route example for the central spawn.

After the opening route: finish your run

Once you have secured the time-sensitive bosses, work through nearby keycard rooms, worthwhile chest spawns, vaults, and any technical chests you want to check. Use Golden Bangboo after kills to search the boss sphere and nearby containers together.

If you plan to fight the Hound in its arena, save Revival Medicine for its damage-negation effect. If Corruption has reduced your maximum HP, use Corruption Cleanse Medicine in a safe moment so the recovery is not interrupted.

Keep watching the timer and the map. When you have the loot you came for, head to an available exit and leave. Only add small chests or extra detours when you have enough time.

Your run at a glance

  1. Prepare your team and the 3 / 9 / 3 / 3 item loadout.
  2. Check your spawn, priority bosses, and portal destinations.
  3. Kill affected bosses before the first Corruption cutoff.
  4. Loot worthwhile containers; transform after boss kills.
  5. Check technical chests if you need blueprints.
  6. Finish your chosen objectives and leave with your loot.
03

Make Sure to Use Portals!

During the roughly 30-second preparation period, open your map and select a cogwheel teleport icon to see where it leads. Portal destinations are fixed, so you can plan around the same connections each run.

EXTRA

How to Get Gold Gear Blueprints

Purple diamonds: The numbered markers on this map show the four technical chests I check for blueprints.

Blueprints come from four specific technical chests marked below. Look for white-gray weapon boxes.

These boxes normally hold two or three items. Look for three hidden items, or four when the chest is in a corrupted zone. The worthwhile combination includes a weapon, a blueprint, and a transformation item.

A short blueprint-focused run

  1. Select Hard difficulty or above.
  2. Bring purple gear; expensive equipment is not required for this approach.
  3. Head for the marked technical chests and check their item counts.
  4. Collect the blueprints, then leave the map through an available exit.

This is an alternative to the full loot route when your main goal is unlocking blueprints.

MAP

Chests and Vaults: Locations

Prioritize boss loot, locked chests, and vaults. Small chests are a lower priority when time is tight. I only check trash bins occasionally for a possible large cash find. I don’t build my route around them.

Read the chest map

Red: Possible locked-chest spawns, influenced by your starting position. These use the timing minigame.

Blue: Guaranteed chest locations.

Yellow: Vaults opened by pressing and holding; no timing minigame.

Your spawn changes the search

Normally, your starting area has no extra locked chests outside keycard-locked rooms. There is one exception at the Bahamut spawn in the bottom-right area. Keycard rooms are still worth checking.

Save time before everything is revealed

I check the number of hidden item slots to decide whether to keep searching:

ContainerWhat you seeWhat I do
VaultOnly three unrevealed/loading slotsSkip: not the high-value loot you are looking for.
Small chestOnly two itemsSkip: usually a purple item plus coins, or two purple items.
Boss loot sphereOnly three itemsSkip to save time (no high value loot)

These are the shortcuts I use to keep my farming runs efficient. If you still need lower-tier supplies, collect what is useful to you. Technical chests use a different slot rule; see “How to Get Gold Gear Blueprints” in the section menu.

START

Understand this spawn

You begin in the central area. The circled spawn zone has no extra locked chests outside keycard-locked rooms, so focus your opening movement on the priority bosses and guaranteed loot.

01 · RED

Clear the upper-right area first

Follow the red path northeast from the spawn and work around the upper-right section. Defeat the marked bosses and loot the small vaults and keycard rooms along the route.

Prioritize the bosses that can transform when their zone becomes corrupted. Securing their loot is the reason to visit this area first.

02 · BLUE

Double back to the gas station

Return along the blue route toward the left side. Clear the boss on the way, then the gas-station area, including the marked loot and keycard room.

Once that section is clear, use the upper-left cogwheel portal. It takes you directly to the bottom-right Bahamut area.

03 · PURPLE

Sweep the lower half of the map

After arriving at the diamond in Bahamut, follow the purple route through the bottom-right area, across the beach, and around the southwest section.

Take the marked boss and loot stops, including keycard rooms. Check worthwhile chest spawns as you pass them and use Golden Bangboo to search clusters efficiently after a kill.

The dashed diagonal purple line represents the teleport journey; the winding purple line is the route you travel on foot.

04 · YELLOW

Finish the remaining western section

Continue from the southwest into the remaining upper-left section along the yellow route. Clear the marked stops and the remaining keycard room, then finish the northern end of the sweep.

At this point, you have completed the illustrated route. Decide whether to finish any remaining objective, fight the Hound, or leave with your loot, based on your time and supplies.

Use the logic when your spawn changes

This exact path is for the illustrated central spawn. With a different start, change your opening order to reach vulnerable bosses before Corruption, account for local chest spawns, and use the fixed portals to reduce travel time.
